Welcome to Sparrow Haven

Sparrow Haven, your home away from home in a relaxed bush environment. Sparrow haven is a charming retreat where relaxation meets adventure and nature!

Here at Sparrow Haven we offer a 2 bedroom house and a separate 1 bedroom flat on the property making it an ideal experience for couples, families or friends looking for an unforgettable bush experience.






The House:

  • 2 Bedrooms: A queen bed and a double bed, both have an ensuite bathroom.
  • Guest toilet
  • Aircons & fans: Stay cool and comfortable in the warm weather.
  • Kitchen: Fully equipped with everything you need in a kitchen.
  • Braai Area: Perfect for outdoor cooking and socializing.
  • Pool and jacuzzi: Relax and unwind in our pool and cold water jacuzzi.
  • Lounge: Cozy up and enjoy our smart TV and board games.
  • Wi-Fi: Stay connected with our free reliable internet.

The Flat:

  • 1 Bedroom: Two single beds.
  • 1 Bathroom: Consists of a bath, shower and toilet.
  • Aircons & fans: Stay cool and comfortable.
  • Kitchen: Fully equipped with everything you need.
  • Braai Area: Enjoy outdoor cooking and relaxation.
  • Smart TV: Enjoy your favorite shows and movies.
  • Jacuzzi: Relax in our cold water jacuzzi.
  • Board Games: Have fun and bond with family and friends
  • Wi-Fi: Stay connected with our free reliable internet.

Booking Options:

  • Flexible Booking: You can book the house or flat separately or combined if traveling in larger groups.
  • Sleeps 2-7 people: Traveling solo or in a group, Sparrow Haven has you sorted.
