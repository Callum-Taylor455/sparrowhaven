Welcome To Sparrow Haven

Sparrow Haven Self Catering

Sparrow Haven, your home away from home in a relaxed bush environment. Sparrow haven is a charming retreat where relaxation meets adventure and nature!

Here at Sparrow Haven we offer a 2 bedroom house and a separate 1 bedroom flat on the property making it an ideal experience for couples, families or friends looking for an unforgettable bush experience.




Accommodation features:

Both the house and flat each have their own braai areas, fans and aircons, braai grids and potjie pot, hot tray, fridge/freezer, extra small chest freezer, gas stove, microwave, kettle and gas stove kettle, toaster, fly protection food screens, fly repellent fans, enough crockery and cutlery, pots and pans, tablecloths, place mats, rechargeable lamps, smart TV and games. The house has a coffee machine (bring your own filters). Flat has a coffee bodum. The main house has a pool and the flat has a 4 man cold water jacuzzi. In adition the house can sleep up to 4 people with 2 optional mattresses and the flat can sleep 2 poeple.

The House:

  • 2 Bedrooms: A queen bed and a double bed, both have an ensuite bathroom.

  • Aircons & fans: Stay cool and comfortable in the warm weather.
